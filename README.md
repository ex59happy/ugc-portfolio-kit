# UGC Portfolio Kit

Build your UGC creator portfolio in about 30 minutes. No coding.

You talk to Claude Code, it asks you a few questions, you drop in your videos,
and you end up with a live link you can send to brands.

---

## Before you start (do this first, it's the whole trick)

Everything below takes 10 minutes and it's the difference between this being
easy and being annoying. Get it done before you open Claude Code.

**1. Get your five roadmap videos.**

These are the five videos you already made following the roadmap. Not your
five best, not anything new. The ones you already have.

**Either the files or the links work.** If you have the video files on your
computer, great. If not, just grab the link to each post and paste those in.

If you are using files, download them from Instagram or TikTok rather than
pulling them off your camera roll. Camera roll videos are a format some
browsers won't play and they're about 10x bigger than they need to be. The
posted versions are already exactly right.

**2. Know which brand each video is for.** You will hand these over together,
one at a time, like "this one's for LaunchPoint" with the link or the file.
Just the brand name. You do not need view counts or results.

**3. Pick 2 photos of yourself.** Ones where you can clearly see your face,
and that look somewhat professional or clean-casual. One goes at the top of
the page (portrait works best), one at the bottom.

**4. Have your email, Instagram and TikTok handles ready.**

**5. Check which brands you've made content for.** Invo, LaunchPoint and
Composio are already built in with their logos, so if you've made a video for
any of them you're done. Making a spec video for a brand counts.

For any other brand, you'll want its logo. Easiest way: open the brand's
Instagram on your phone, screenshot the profile picture, crop it square.

---

## Building it

Open Claude Code and paste this in:

```
Build my UGC creator portfolio.

Make a folder called ugc-portfolio on my Desktop and put the template from
github.com/ex59happy/ugc-portfolio-kit directly inside it, so that CLAUDE.md
and index.html sit at the top level of that folder. Then read CLAUDE.md and
follow it exactly.
```

That's the only thing you have to copy. Claude takes it from there: it'll ask
you questions, open the right folders for you to drag your files into, build
the site, show it to you, and put it online.

---

## What you end up with

A four section portfolio:

1. **Hero** with your name, title, contact button and links
2. **Trusted by** logo strip
3. **UGC Examples** with your videos in phone frames, playing automatically
4. **Work With Me** with your photo and contact details

Works on phones. Brands will open it on their phone.

---

## Changing it later

Open the `ugc-portfolio` folder in Claude Code again and tell it what you want
different. "Swap videos 2 and 3." "Add a new brand." "Change my email."

You never have to touch the code.

---

## Does this cost anything?

No. The template is free, and putting it online with Vercel is free.

The only thing you'd ever pay for is a custom domain like `yourname.com`,
which runs about $12 a year and is completely optional. A free
`yourname.vercel.app` link works fine for brand outreach.
