Brand logos for the "Trusted by" strip on your site.

Invo, LaunchPoint and Composio are already in here. If you have made
a video for any of them, you are already done.

To add another brand, drop its logo in this folder. Easiest way:
open the brand's Instagram on your phone, screenshot the profile
picture, and crop it square.

Every brand needs a real logo. A brand name on its own looks like
something you made up.
